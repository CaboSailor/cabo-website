About this experience

Join our popular shared sunset sailing tour and experience the magic of Cabo San Lucas as day turns to night. Sail past the famous Arch, toast with fellow adventurers, and watch one of the world's most spectacular sunsets from the deck of a beautiful sailing yacht. Our experienced bilingual crew ensures your comfort throughout the journey.

The Experience

  1. 1Meet at the marina & board the sailing yacht
  2. 2Cruise past Cabo's highlights — the Arch, sea lions, and Lover's Beach
  3. 3Settle into the best views as the sun begins to drop
  4. 4Enjoy golden hour with unlimited drinks and light bites
  5. 5Watch the sun set over the Pacific, then cruise back along the coastline

No rush. No fixed script. Just a relaxed, beautiful time on the water.

Sunset at Land's End

Land's End at sunset is one of the most photographed and celebrated moments in all of Mexico. As the sun descends toward the Pacific horizon, the sky transforms into a canvas of amber, rose, and violet — with the iconic Arch silhouetted in the foreground.

Our crew positions the boat at the perfect vantage point so every guest has an unobstructed view of this nightly spectacle. It's a moment you'll carry with you long after you leave Cabo.
