FAQ

Everything You Need to Know Before Booking

What's included in the cruise?expand_more

All cruises include crew, open bar, snorkeling gear, and light food or snacks. Private charters can be customized based on your preferences.

Is the cruise private or shared?expand_more

We offer both options. Private charters are exclusively for your group, while shared cruises are limited to small groups (max 12 guests) for a more comfortable experience.

How many people are on board?expand_more

Private cruises vary by your group (up to 18–20 guests depending on the yacht). Shared cruises are intentionally limited to around 14 guests to avoid crowds.

What is your cancellation policy?expand_more

Free cancellation or changes up to 48 hours before departure.

50% refund between 48–24 hours.

No refund within 24 hours or for no-shows.

What happens in case of bad weather?expand_more

Safety comes first. If conditions are not suitable, we'll reschedule your cruise or provide a full refund.

Why choose you over other Cabo cruises?expand_more

We operate our own fleet, limit group sizes, and focus on personalized service—offering a more comfortable and premium experience than typical crowded tours.
