A babymoon is your last vacation as a couple before the baby arrives &mdash; and Cabo San Lucas is one of the best places to take it. Year-round sunshine, calm waters, gorgeous scenery, and a short flight from most US cities make it an easy, stress-free choice for expecting parents.

Here's how to plan a Cabo babymoon in 2026, including which activities are pregnancy-safe, the best resorts for a quieter vibe, and what to skip.

---

## When to go (trimester timing)

<div class="bg-blue-50 border border-blue-200 rounded-xl p-6 my-6">
  <p class="font-bold text-blue-800 text-lg mb-2">The second trimester (weeks 14&ndash;28) is the sweet spot</p>
  <p class="text-blue-900 text-sm mb-2">Most OB-GYNs recommend traveling during the second trimester, when:</p>
  <ul class="text-blue-900 text-sm space-y-1">
    <li>&bull; Morning sickness has usually subsided</li>
    <li>&bull; Energy levels are higher than the third trimester</li>
    <li>&bull; Most airlines allow travel without a doctor's note until 36 weeks</li>
    <li>&bull; You're comfortable enough to enjoy activities, walks, and dining out</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="text-blue-900 text-sm mt-2 mb-0"><strong>Always check with your doctor before booking</strong> &mdash; every pregnancy is different.</p>
</div>

## Best babymoon-friendly resorts

These resorts are quieter, more couples-oriented, and offer good spa and dining options:

<ul class="spot-list">
  <li><strong>The Cape, a Thompson Hotel</strong> &mdash; boutique luxury on the Pacific side with a rooftop restaurant and stunning Arch views. Adults-only pool area.</li>
  <li><strong>Montage Los Cabos</strong> &mdash; spacious suites, world-class spa with prenatal treatments, and a calm beach setting in the Tourist Corridor.</li>
  <li><strong>Zadun, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve</strong> &mdash; ultra-quiet, intimate, and focused on wellness. One of the most exclusive properties in Los Cabos.</li>
  <li><strong>Chileno Bay Resort (Auberge)</strong> &mdash; set on one of Cabo's only swimmable beaches. Relaxed atmosphere, excellent restaurants.</li>
  <li><strong>Marquis Los Cabos</strong> &mdash; all-suite, adults-only, with a holistic spa. Great value compared to the ultra-luxury tier.</li>
</ul>

<div class="bg-green-50 border border-green-200 rounded-xl p-6 my-6">
  <p class="text-green-900 text-sm mb-0"><strong>Tip:</strong> when booking, mention you're on a babymoon. Many resorts offer complimentary upgrades, spa credits, or room decorations for expecting couples.</p>

## What to pack

<ul class="spot-list">
  <li><strong>Comfortable swimwear</strong> &mdash; maternity swimsuits with good support</li>
  <li><strong>Loose, breathable clothing</strong> &mdash; Cabo is warm year-round, and you'll want to stay cool</li>
  <li><strong>High-SPF reef-safe sunscreen</strong> &mdash; pregnancy can increase sun sensitivity</li>
  <li><strong>Prenatal vitamins + any medications</strong> &mdash; bring enough for the trip plus a few extra days</li>
  <li><strong>Compression socks</strong> &mdash; recommended for the flight, especially in the third trimester</li>
  <li><strong>A copy of your doctor's note</strong> &mdash; not always required, but good to have for airlines and activities</li>
</ul>

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Cabo safe for pregnant travelers?**

Yes. Cabo has excellent private healthcare facilities, and the tourist area is safe and easy to navigate. Stick to bottled water and well-cooked food as you would anywhere in Mexico.

**Can I go on a boat tour while pregnant?**

A calm-water sunset cruise or whale watching tour is generally fine during the second trimester. Avoid high-speed boats and rough-water excursions. Always check with your OB-GYN first.

**What should I skip in Cabo while pregnant?**

Scuba diving, jet skis, ATVs, zip-lining, and anything with a high risk of impact or falls. Also skip hot tubs, saunas, and uncooked seafood (ceviche).

**When is the best time for a Cabo babymoon?**

November through May offers the best weather &mdash; warm, dry, and sunny. If you want to combine it with whale watching, visit December through April.
