The Los Cabos Open of Surf is one of Mexico&rsquo;s biggest surf competitions and the largest action-sports festival in Latin America. Held at the legendary Zippers Beach in San Jos&eacute; del Cabo, the event draws professional surfers from around the world and tens of thousands of spectators for a week of competition, concerts, food, and beachside culture.

Here&rsquo;s everything you need to know to attend.

---

## History and background

The event launched in 2013 as an ASP (now WSL) 6-star qualifying event, immediately establishing Cabo as a serious surf destination. Over the years, it has grown well beyond surfing &mdash; hosting live music from major Mexican and international artists, a food fair with Baja cuisine, fashion shows, skateboarding exhibitions, and beach volleyball tournaments.

Notable surfers who have competed include Carissa Moore, Tatiana Weston-Webb, and several Mexican national champions. The event typically runs for 5&ndash;7 days in June and attracts 50,000+ visitors over the course of the festival.

---

## Where is Zippers Beach?

Zippers (officially part of Costa Azul) is located on the Pacific side of San Jos&eacute; del Cabo, about 25 minutes from Cabo San Lucas and 10 minutes from downtown San Jos&eacute;.

<ul class="spot-list">
  <li><strong>Location</strong> &mdash; Playa Costa Azul, along the Tourist Corridor between Cabo San Lucas and San Jos&eacute; del Cabo</li>
  <li><strong>Access</strong> &mdash; the beach is public. During the event, designated viewing areas are set up on the sand</li>
  <li><strong>Nearby landmark</strong> &mdash; The Cape, a Thompson Hotel, sits right on the beach at Zippers and offers VIP viewing options</li>
</ul>

---

## What to expect at the event

The Los Cabos Open of Surf is more than a surf contest &mdash; it&rsquo;s a full beach festival:

<ul class="spot-list">
  <li><strong>Professional surf competition</strong> &mdash; WSL-sanctioned heats running throughout the day. The best viewing is from the beach, right at the waterline</li>
  <li><strong>Live concerts</strong> &mdash; major artists perform evening shows on the beach stage. Past headliners have included popular Latin rock and reggae acts</li>
  <li><strong>Food fair</strong> &mdash; local restaurants and food trucks set up along the beach with tacos, ceviche, craft beer, and cocktails</li>
  <li><strong>Fashion and art</strong> &mdash; surf and streetwear brands showcase collections, and local artists display work</li>
  <li><strong>Skateboarding and beach volleyball</strong> &mdash; side competitions and exhibitions throughout the week</li>
</ul>

## What to know before you go

<div class="bg-blue-50 border border-blue-200 rounded-xl p-6 my-6">
  <p class="font-bold text-blue-800 text-lg mb-2">Spectator tips</p>
  <ul class="text-blue-900 text-sm space-y-1">
    <li>&bull; <strong>Dates</strong> &mdash; typically held in June. Check the official Los Cabos Open of Surf social channels for exact dates</li>
    <li>&bull; <strong>Entry</strong> &mdash; beach viewing of the surf competition is <strong>free</strong>. Some concerts and VIP areas may require tickets</li>
    <li>&bull; <strong>Arrive early</strong> &mdash; parking is limited along Costa Azul. Get there by 9 AM on competition days or take an Uber</li>
    <li>&bull; <strong>Bring</strong> &mdash; sunscreen, a hat, water, and cash for food vendors. There&rsquo;s little shade on the beach</li>
    <li>&bull; <strong>Hotels book up</strong> &mdash; if you&rsquo;re planning to visit during the event, book accommodations at least 2&ndash;3 months in advance</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## Things to do nearby

If you&rsquo;re in the area for the Open of Surf, make time for:

<ul class="spot-list">
  <li><strong>Surf lessons at Cerritos Beach</strong> &mdash; about 45 minutes north toward Todos Santos. Gentle, beginner-friendly waves and several surf schools right on the sand</li>
  <li><strong>San Jos&eacute; del Cabo Art Walk</strong> &mdash; Thursday evenings from November to June. Galleries, live music, and street food in the historic art district</li>
  <li><strong>Dinner in San Jos&eacute;</strong> &mdash; the downtown restaurant scene is excellent. Farm-to-table Baja cuisine, seafood, and mezcal bars</li>
  <li><strong>Palmilla Beach</strong> &mdash; a calmer swimming beach just down the coast, perfect for a mellow morning before heading to the competition</li>
</ul>

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Los Cabos Open of Surf free to attend?**

The surf competition on the beach is free to watch &mdash; just show up and find a spot on the sand. Some evening concerts and VIP areas may have separate admission, but the core event is open to the public.

**Where exactly is Zippers Beach?**

Zippers is part of Playa Costa Azul, on the Pacific coast of San Jos&eacute; del Cabo. It&rsquo;s along the Tourist Corridor, roughly halfway between Cabo San Lucas and downtown San Jos&eacute;. The Cape hotel is the main landmark.

**Can beginners surf at Zippers?**

Zippers is an advanced break with a rocky bottom and powerful waves &mdash; it&rsquo;s not suitable for beginners. If you want to try surfing, head to Cerritos Beach near Todos Santos, which has sandy bottom, gentle waves, and several surf schools.
