Traveling from La Paz to Cabo San Lucas is more than just getting from A to B &mdash; it's a journey through some of the most stunning landscapes in Baja California Sur. The two cities are about 155 km (97 miles) apart along Highway 1, but they offer very different experiences. Cabo is known for its lively resorts and nightlife, while La Paz has a more relaxed, authentic Mexican vibe.

Whether you're on a tight schedule or want to explore along the way, here are the best ways to make the trip in 2026.

## Rental car &mdash; freedom to explore

Renting a car is the most flexible option. The drive from La Paz to Cabo takes about two hours on Mexico Highway 1 and gives you the freedom to stop wherever you like &mdash; the artist town of Todos Santos, Balandra Beach, or the Sierra de la Laguna foothills.

Car rentals in La Paz and Los Cabos typically start around $60 per day. Make sure to choose a plan with unlimited mileage to avoid surprise charges. Mexican liability insurance is required by law and is usually an add-on at the rental counter.

---

## Aguila or ABC bus &mdash; the budget-friendly choice

For budget travelers, the bus is the cheapest way to get from La Paz to Cabo. Two main companies &mdash; **Autobuses de la Baja California (ABC)** and **Aguila** &mdash; run regular routes between the two cities. The ride takes about three hours and costs around $17&ndash;$20 USD one way.

In Cabo San Lucas the bus terminal is a short walk from the marina. In La Paz, buses depart from the Central de Autobuses near the Malec&oacute;n. Buses are reliable and easy to access, though they can get crowded during peak season.

---

## Shared shuttle &mdash; comfort and affordability

If driving isn't for you, a shared shuttle is a great middle ground. These services run regularly between La Paz and Cabo, offering a comfortable ride without the hassle of navigating or parking. The trip usually takes around 2 to 2.5 hours and costs roughly $30&ndash;$50 per person.

Shared shuttles are a solid option for solo travelers or small groups. Some operators offer round-trip discounts and hotel pickup, making the return journey just as easy.

## Taxi &mdash; door-to-door without a booking

You can negotiate a taxi directly at the La Paz airport or from the city center. A one-way taxi to Cabo typically runs around $150&ndash;$180 USD. The price is fixed per vehicle, not per person, so splitting with travel companions brings the cost down.

Taxis are convenient when you need a ride immediately without pre-booking, though you won't have the same price guarantees as a shuttle or transfer service.

---

## Private transfer &mdash; convenience and comfort

For travelers who want a door-to-door experience with no surprises, a <a href="/shuttle-service/" class="text-accent-gold font-semibold hover:underline">private transfer</a> is the premium option. A driver picks you up at your hotel or the airport in La Paz and drops you directly at your Cabo accommodation. The journey takes about two hours and you can request stops along the way.

Private transfers typically cost $180&ndash;$200 one way. The price includes the vehicle, driver, and fuel &mdash; no hidden fees. This option is ideal for families, groups, or anyone arriving on a late flight.

---

## Which option is right for you?

The best choice depends on your budget, group size, and travel style. Budget travelers will love the bus. Couples and solo travelers may prefer a shared shuttle. Families and groups get the most value from a private transfer or rental car. And road-trip lovers should absolutely rent a car to explore the coastline at their own pace.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there Uber in La Paz?**

No. As of 2026, Uber does not operate in La Paz or anywhere in Baja California Sur. Your best options are local taxis, pre-booked shuttles, or private transfer services.

**Is there a train from La Paz to Cabo?**

No. There is no passenger rail service in Baja California Sur. The only land-based options are bus, shuttle, taxi, rental car, or private transfer along Highway 1.

**Can I do La Paz and Cabo in one day?**

Yes, but it will be a long day. The drive is about two hours each way. If you leave Cabo early, you can spend 4&ndash;5 hours exploring the La Paz Malec&oacute;n and Balandra Beach before heading back. A shared shuttle or rental car works best for a day trip.

**What's the cheapest option?**

The Aguila or ABC bus at around $20 one way. It's reliable and runs multiple times per day, though the ride takes about three hours and comfort is basic.

**What's the most comfortable option?**

A private transfer. A driver picks you up at your door, the vehicle is air-conditioned, and you can request scenic stops along the way. It costs around $200 but the convenience is hard to beat &mdash; especially for families or groups splitting the fare.
