La Paz and Cabo San Lucas are both in Baja California Sur, only two hours apart by car &mdash; but they feel like completely different destinations. Cabo is the famous resort town with nightlife, luxury hotels, and a party scene. La Paz is the state capital &mdash; a working Mexican city with a beautiful malec&oacute;n, stunning natural beaches, and a fraction of the tourist crowds.

Here&rsquo;s how they compare across every category that matters.

## Getting there

**Cabo San Lucas** is served by San Jos&eacute; del Cabo International Airport (SJD), with direct flights from most major US and Canadian cities. The airport is about 20 minutes from Cabo and 10 minutes from San Jos&eacute; del Cabo.

**La Paz** has its own airport (LAP) with limited domestic flights, but most international visitors fly into SJD and drive to La Paz. The drive is approximately 2 hours on a modern highway. For details on the route, see our guide to <a href="/travel-guide/convenient-ways-to-get-from-la-paz-to-cabo-for-every-type-of-traveler/" class="text-accent-gold font-semibold hover:underline">getting from La Paz to Cabo</a>.

## Beaches

Cabo&rsquo;s best beaches &mdash; Medano, Chileno Bay, and Santa Mar&iacute;a Bay &mdash; are beautiful and well-serviced, but they draw crowds, especially in high season.

La Paz wins on beaches by a wide margin. **Balandra** is regularly ranked among the most beautiful beaches in Mexico &mdash; a shallow, turquoise lagoon surrounded by desert hills, with almost no development. **Tecolote** is a long, wide beach on the Sea of Cortez with rustic restaurants and calm water. Both feel completely untouched compared to Cabo&rsquo;s resort-lined shores.

---

## Activities

<ul class="spot-list">
  <li><strong>Cabo</strong> &mdash; snorkeling at Pelican Rock, sailing past the Arch, sunset cruises, deep-sea fishing, whale watching, ATV tours, zip-lining, nightlife</li>
  <li><strong>La Paz</strong> &mdash; swimming with whale sharks (Oct&ndash;Apr), sea lion snorkeling at Isla Esp&iacute;ritu Santo, diving at El Bajo seamount, kayaking at Balandra, malec&oacute;n sunset walks</li>
</ul>

Cabo offers a wider range of organized tours and entertainment. La Paz offers more raw nature and unique wildlife encounters &mdash; the whale shark and sea lion swims are world-class experiences you can&rsquo;t replicate in Cabo.

---

## Dining

<ul class="spot-list">
  <li><strong>Cabo</strong> &mdash; upscale resort dining, international cuisine, famous chef-driven restaurants, a party-oriented bar scene</li>
  <li><strong>La Paz</strong> &mdash; fresh seafood at local mariscos restaurants, market tacos, chocolate clam specialties, mezcal bars on the malec&oacute;n. Less polished but more authentic</li>
</ul>

La Paz&rsquo;s food scene punches above its weight. The city is known for its **chocolate clam** (almeja chocolata), served raw with lime and chili or grilled with butter and garlic. Seafood is fresher and cheaper across the board.

---

## Nightlife

Cabo&rsquo;s nightlife is famous &mdash; Squid Roe, El Squid Roe, Cabo Wabo, Mandala, and a strip of clubs that keep going until the early hours. If you&rsquo;re looking for a party, this is it.

La Paz&rsquo;s nightlife is quieter and more grown-up &mdash; malec&oacute;n bars, live music, craft cocktail spots, and mezcaler&iacute;as. It&rsquo;s a place for a long dinner and drinks, not a club night.

---

## Accommodations

<ul class="spot-list">
  <li><strong>Cabo</strong> &mdash; major international hotel brands, all-inclusive resorts, luxury villas, and high-end vacation rentals. This is where the big resorts are</li>
  <li><strong>La Paz</strong> &mdash; boutique hotels, locally-owned inns, and Airbnbs at significantly lower prices. A nice hotel in La Paz costs what a budget hotel costs in Cabo</li>
</ul>

## Can you do both?

Yes &mdash; and we recommend it if you have at least 5 days. La Paz is about a 2-hour drive north of Cabo on a well-maintained highway. Many visitors split their trip: a few days in La Paz for nature and quiet, then a few days in Cabo for resort life and nightlife.

For details on getting between the two, including bus options, shuttles, and driving, see our full guide: <a href="/travel-guide/convenient-ways-to-get-from-la-paz-to-cabo-for-every-type-of-traveler/" class="text-accent-gold font-semibold hover:underline">Convenient Ways to Get from La Paz to Cabo</a>.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is cheaper &mdash; La Paz or Cabo?**

La Paz is significantly cheaper. Hotels run 30&ndash;50% less, restaurant meals cost about half what you&rsquo;d pay in Cabo, and activities like whale shark tours are priced for a broader market. A budget day in La Paz can run $30&ndash;$50 per person compared to $60&ndash;$100 in Cabo.

**Can you do both La Paz and Cabo in one trip?**

Absolutely. They&rsquo;re only 2 hours apart by car. A common itinerary is 2&ndash;3 days in La Paz followed by 2&ndash;3 days in Cabo (or vice versa). Rent a car at SJD airport and drive to La Paz first, then return to Cabo for the second half.

**Which destination has better beaches?**

La Paz. Balandra is consistently rated one of Mexico&rsquo;s most beautiful beaches &mdash; a turquoise lagoon with almost no one on it. Cabo&rsquo;s beaches are lovely but more developed and crowded. For pure beach beauty and tranquility, La Paz wins.

**Is La Paz safe for tourists?**

Yes. La Paz is consistently ranked among the safest cities in Mexico. It&rsquo;s a mid-size state capital with a low crime rate, a large expat community, and a strong tourism infrastructure. Standard travel precautions apply, but violent crime affecting tourists is extremely rare.
