## Experience the Beauty of Los Cabos with the Pelican Rock Cabo Snorkeling Tour

A journey into the heart of Cabo bay with a Pelican Rock Cabo Snorkeling Tour is a perfect choice. This tour is an opportunity to explore, under the waves, the stunning bay of Cabo San Lucas.
On a <a href="/shared-snorkeling/" class="text-accent-gold font-semibold hover:underline">Cabo snorkeling tour</a>, you will discover the wonders of the Sea of Cortez. This body of water is one of the most beautiful in Mexico and the world.

---

## Day Sails and boat tours to Pelican Rock

Day sail and bout tours to Pelican Rock start with daily departures from Cabo marina and into the Bay. Whether <a href="/private-snorkeling/" class="text-accent-gold font-semibold hover:underline">private snorkeling tours</a> or shared those leave daily.
Boarding the vessel, you will be welcomed by the friendly crew of Cabo Sailing. Expect to be well looked after.
The welcoming personnel will provide fresh towels, snorkel gear, swimming aids, and refreshments. Sit down, lay back, and relax on comfortable cushions. The smooth waters of Cabo San Lucas bay are leading you now on an eventful journey.

---

## Cool Off from the Hot Sun with Cabo Snorkeling Tours

Setting sail, approaching, and anchoring at the Pelican Rock, take the opportunity to cool off from the hot sun and jump in. Take in the breathtaking scenery of the southernmost tip of the Baja Peninsula.
The <a href="/travel-guide/snorkeling-chileno-bay/" class="text-accent-gold font-semibold hover:underline">Sea of Cortez</a> is a treasure trove of marine life. Now can witness its aquatic life firsthand. Expect to see colorful fish and corals, sea lions and turtles, and Mobula Rays all in vibrant colors.

---

## Snorkeling in Clearwater

The first stop will be Pelican Rock, a stunning rock formation rising out of the clear waters of Cabo bay.
Our experienced local guides will provide snorkeling aids to keep you floating effortlessly. The crew will point you to the best spots to find fascinating locations around the rock.
Jumping in snorkeling in the clear water, discover the marine life that calls this place home.

## Pelican Rock &mdash; The Ultimate Destination for Swimming and Snorkeling in Los Cabos

### Comfortable, safe environment for swimming and snorkeling tour

As this post suggests, Pelican Rock is not only a stunning rock formation but also a fantastic spot for swimming and snorkeling.
The crystal-clear waters surrounding the rock are teeming with a variety of marine life, making it an ideal destination for underwater exploration.
In addition, the smooth waters surrounding the rock provide a comfortable and safe environment for swimming.

---

## A splash away from Lover's Beach

Located near Lover's Beach, Pelican Rock is easily accessible from the beach. And can be reached by a short boat ride. (or even a swim if you are strong enough).
Visitors can enjoy water splashing activities in the area, and use snorkeling and swimming gear aids to enhance the experience. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced swimmer, Pelican Rock has something for everyone.
Don't miss the chance to immerse yourself in the breathtaking beauty of the natural wonder. Enjoy a refreshing swim or snorkel in Cabo San Lucas bay.
