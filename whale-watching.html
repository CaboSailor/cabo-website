About this experience

Every winter, thousands of humpback and gray whales migrate to the warm waters off Cabo San Lucas — and our private charters put you right in their path. Watch in wonder as these gentle giants breach, slap their tails, and sing — sometimes just meters from the boat. Our experienced naturalist crew knows exactly where to find them, and follows responsible whale-watching practices to keep every encounter safe and respectful.

The Experience

  1. 1Meet at the marina & board your private boat
  2. 2Cruise past the Arch and the sea lion colony
  3. 3Head out to whale territory with your naturalist guide
  4. 4Watch humpbacks and grays breach, sing & tail-slap
  5. 5Relax onboard with drinks, lunch & great views

No rush. No fixed script. Just a great time on the water with the whales.

Frequently Asked Questions

Whale season in Cabo runs from mid-December to mid-April. Morning tours often have calmer waters, while afternoon winds can sometimes lead to more active whale behavior.

The most common sightings are humpback and gray whales. Occasionally, visitors may spot blue whales, orcas, or whale sharks in the surrounding waters.

Most whales in Cabo migrate from the cold waters of Alaska and Canada. They travel thousands of miles to the warm lagoons of Baja to mate and give birth.

We strictly follow environmental guidelines to minimize impact. This includes maintaining safe distances, limiting observation time, and avoiding any disruption to their natural behavior.

The Whales of Cabo

Whales are mammals that breathe air and nurse their young. Humpbacks are known for their spectacular breaching and complex songs that can travel for miles underwater. Most whales in Cabo migrate from the cold waters of Alaska and Canada — they travel thousands of miles to the warm lagoons of Baja to mate and give birth.

The most common sightings here are humpback and gray whales. Occasionally, you may also spot blue whales, orcas, or whale sharks in the surrounding waters.
